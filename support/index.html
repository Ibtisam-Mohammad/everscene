Support

Answers to the usual questions. Anything else, just write: we read every message.

How do I set a scene as my wallpaper?

Open Everscene, tap a scene, then tap Use this scene. The first time, Android shows a preview: tap Set wallpaper. After that, you can switch scenes in the app and they change in place.

What makes a scene react?

Plugging in your charger, shaking the phone, tapping the screen, and the real time of day. Each scene has its own reactions; the scene's page in the app lists them, with buttons to try each one.

I bought a scene. How do I get it on a new phone?

Sign in to Google Play with the same Google account and open Everscene: your scenes are unlocked again automatically. If Android's backup is on, your settings and progress come back too.

A purchase hasn't unlocked, or it says "payment pending".

Some payment methods take a while to confirm; the scene unlocks as soon as Google Play confirms it. If it still hasn't after a day, open the app once with an internet connection so Google Play can update, then write to us with the date of the purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Google Play. You can request one from your Google Play order history; within 48 hours of buying, it's usually automatic.

Does it drain my battery?

Scenes move only while you can see them and rest when the screen is off. With battery saver on, they slow down further. If you notice heavy use on your phone, please tell us its model.

The wallpaper went back to the phone's default.

Some phones reset live wallpapers after an update or to save power. Open Everscene and tap Use this scene again. If it keeps happening, tell us your phone's model and we'll look into it.

Does Everscene collect my data?

No. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and what the app uses stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.
